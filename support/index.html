How can we help?

Need help with Roza — Cycle Sync Fitness? We're here for you.

Contact us

Email: hello.rozaapp@gmail.com

Typical response time: 48–72 hours.

Subscription & billing

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To view or cancel yours, open Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Roza on your iPhone.

Restore a purchase

If you reinstalled the app or changed devices, open Roza → Settings → Restore Purchase.

App crashing or bugs

Email us and include:

  • Your device model (e.g. iPhone 13)
  • Your iOS version
  • What happened before the issue

Suggestions & feedback

We'd love to hear from you. Send feedback anytime.
