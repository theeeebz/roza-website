Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Roza (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Roza mobile application (the “App”) keeps on your device, what it sends to the service providers that help us run it, and the choices you have.

In short: everything you log about your cycle, your body and your training stays on your device. To understand how the App is used, measure our ads and manage your subscription, the App sends a limited set of usage events to Amplitude, Meta and RevenueCat, described below. We never sell your data.

2. Information That Stays on Your Device

The following is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to any third party:

  • Your cycle information: period dates, cycle length and cycle phase
  • Your symptoms, notes and energy check-ins
  • Your workouts and training history
  • Your profile: your display name and profile photo

We do not store this information on our servers and do not back it up. If you delete the App, it is permanently lost. Your device’s own backups, such as an iCloud backup of your iPhone, may include it, under Apple’s terms.

3. Information Sent to Service Providers

The App sends the information below to three service providers. None of it includes the information listed in section 2.

a. Amplitude (product analytics)

We use Amplitude to understand how the App is used and to improve it. The App sends:

  • Events describing what you do in the App, such as reaching an onboarding step, starting and ending a workout, seeing the paywall, or answering the rating prompt
  • That you logged your energy, your symptoms or your period, without what you logged
  • Your answers to onboarding and workout setup: your interests (for example strength training or weight loss), training days per week, training location, level and session length
  • A random identifier for your installation and a random user identifier, which links your usage to your subscription
  • Basic device and app information, such as device model, operating system, app version and language

The App does not send Amplitude your IP address, city, carrier or Apple’s vendor identifier (IDFV), and it does not record what you tap or type.

b. Meta (ad measurement)

We use Meta’s SDK to measure whether our ads on Meta’s platforms lead to installs. The App sends Meta only these events:

  • That the App was opened (the first time counts as the install)
  • That you completed onboarding
  • That you started a free trial
  • That you subscribed, with the plan’s price and currency

Along with them, Meta receives an anonymous identifier for your installation and the technical information any network request carries, such as your IP address, device model and operating system. The App does not ask for permission to track you, does not access your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA), and has Meta’s automatic event logging turned off. Meta also processes this information under its own Privacy Policy.

c. RevenueCat (subscriptions)

Purchases are made through Apple. We never see your payment details. RevenueCat manages your subscription and receives the transaction information Apple provides, such as the product, price, dates and store country, together with a random user identifier. RevenueCat may forward subscription events, such as purchases, renewals and cancellations, to Amplitude, and renewals and refunds to Meta, linked to the identifiers described above.

d. Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and use them only to reply.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the App’s features, which run on your device
  • To understand how the App is used and improve it (Amplitude)
  • To measure the performance of our advertising (Meta)
  • To provide and manage your subscription (RevenueCat and Apple)
  • To answer your messages

We do not use your information for targeted advertising, and we do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

5. Legal Basis

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we process your information on the following bases:

  • Performance of our contract with you, to provide the App and your subscription
  • Our legitimate interests in understanding and improving the App and measuring our advertising. You can object at any time (see section 8)

6. International Transfers

Amplitude, Meta and RevenueCat process information in the United States. Where required, these transfers rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

7. Data Retention

  • Information on your device remains until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App.
  • Analytics data in Amplitude is kept for as long as it is useful to improve the App, and is deleted on request.
  • Subscription records in RevenueCat are kept as long as needed to manage your subscription and meet legal obligations.
  • Meta keeps the information it receives under its own retention policies.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw any consent you have given. To exercise these rights, email us at hello.rozaapp@gmail.com. Because the App has no accounts, we may ask for information to help us find the data linked to your installation.

Information stored on your device is under your control: you can delete it in the App or by uninstalling the App.

If you are in the European Economic Area, you may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. In France, this is the CNIL (cnil.fr).

9. Children’s Privacy

Roza is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated “Last updated” date.
